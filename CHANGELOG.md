# Changelog

All notable changes to Blueprint Sketch. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## 0.20.0 — 2026-10-18
- Works on phones: the viewer fills the screen, tools float over it, the panel opens as a bottom sheet and the camera pad appears on demand.
- Fixed: drawing with a finger or a pen no longer breaks when the browser refuses to capture the pointer (0.19.1).

## 0.19.0 — 2026-10-17
- New *Boceto* look: white scene with black outlines, like an architectural sketch.
- New finishes: dark oak and polished concrete floors, exposed-concrete walls, and wood-batten or concrete ceilings per room.
- New people silhouette to give scale.

## 0.18 — 2026-10-17
- Version history for each plan: named versions, automatic checkpoints, restore, comparison and copy as a new plan (0.18.0).
- Every version carries a content hash and its capture time (0.18.1).
- The top bar shows the current version with share, export and full-screen options, and announces automatic checkpoints (0.18.2).

## 0.17 — 2026-10-17
- Assistant tab and MCP server: a language model of your choice draws plans with the editor's own tools (0.17.0).
- Panel tabs stacked in a vertical column (0.17.1).

## 0.16 — 2026-10-17
- Resize furniture to standard sizes within sensible limits (0.16.0).
- Upper levels are visible in every view but the plan (0.16.1).

## 0.15.0 — 2026-10-17
- Photorealistic look with sky, soft shadows and fog, and saving the view as an image.

## 0.13 – 0.14 — 2026-10-17
- Gallery of 30 ready-made plans when a workspace starts: homes, buildings, offices, shops, schools, parks and more.

## 0.12.0 — 2026-10-17
- Layers for walls, openings, floors, stairs, furniture and dimensions, plus your own, with hide, lock and DXF export.

## 0.11 — 2026-10-17
- Professional-style layout with modes, tools, panels and an on-canvas camera pad (0.11.0).
- Perspective menu with aerial angles and a view inside each room (0.11.1).

## 0.10 — 2026-10-17
- Several levels connected by stairs, drawn, exported and budgeted level by level, with a view through the floors.

## 0.8 – 0.9 — 2026-10-17
- Furnish with your own imported 3D models (.glb), which also travel with shared plans.

## 0.7 — 2026-10-17
- Quantities and budget tab with prices, contingency and CSV export (0.7.0).
- Fewer saves to stay within the free storage quota (0.7.1).
- Owners and editors open shared plans ready to build (0.7.2).

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-17
- Export the plan as a PDF or DXF drawing to scale.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-10-17
- Type exact measurements for walls, openings, floors and furniture.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-17
- Undo and redo every change.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-16
- Share a plan on its own link with owner, editor and reader roles.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-16
- Start from an empty plan with draw, move and reshape tools.

## 0.1 — 2026-10-16
- First version: a 2D plan and a 3D editor.
